# Abstract

We propose a **Dual-Space Optimization Model (DSOM)** that performs a two-stage adaptation process: first aligning the feature space via backbone normalization, and then refining prototype embeddings through energy-space optimization. DSOM leverages progressive pseudo-labeling to dynamically update normalization parameters and employs energy regularization with prior-anchored prototypes to enhance class-wise discrimination while providing energy-based uncertainty estimation for open-set detection.
